# Vendor payout requirement

Confirmed by the project owner: payouts are performed by the admin, and every vendor payout requires admin approval.

- Admins review eligible vendor earnings and approve or reject payouts.
- Vendors can view their payout status and history; they cannot release funds.
- Approval authorizes payment; it does not mean the transfer has completed.
- The admin performs the transfer and records its payment reference. Mark a payout paid only after confirmed transfer.
- Vendors cannot approve their own payouts or mark them paid.
- Record the approving admin, decision time, amount, and rejection reason when applicable.

Do not automatically transfer money to vendors. Vendor payout requests are not a confirmed requirement.

This is a recorded requirement. The admin payout workflow is not implemented yet.
